# Decisions

## Decision Log

### YYYY-MM-DD: Decision title

- **Status:** proposed | accepted | superseded
- **Context:** What required a decision.
- **Decision:** What was chosen.
- **Alternatives:** Other options considered.
- **Reason:** Why this option was selected.
- **Consequences:** Expected tradeoffs and follow-up work.

## Open Questions

- Record unresolved questions, owners when known, and what evidence will answer them.
